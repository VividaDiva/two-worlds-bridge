Group dynamics, measured

Two AI players with hidden needs talk; a third AI builds a bridge from what it hears. Changing only the communication path changes whose needs survive into the thing that gets built. These charts read the 160 simulated sessions that ran on the same eight paths as the live human study.

Reading these as a negotiation study. Fingerprints show process (who gets the floor, when state changes). The heatmap and dumbbell show outcome by condition. The fate chart links the two: it separates needs that travelled through the channel from needs the builder supplied on its own. The anatomy grid shows the design space actually explored. Together they answer the three questions a multi-agent negotiation visualisation has to answer: who spoke, who was heard, and what came out.
