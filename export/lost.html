Where it was lost

The first version of the study, without the toolkit. Each line a person said was coded into at most three keys from a list of fifteen, and only those keys reached the builder. Two things were lost on the way: the words, and then the needs.

3 · Keys = switch positions · the builder's eight switches, and the name each position gets

The 15 keys are not categories of need: each one is the name of a switch position. Hearing a key means flipping that switch; the crossing "has" a key when its switch sits there. minimal = every switch at its first position. water / rock describe the ground, not the bridge. The filled dot is where this run's crossing ended.
