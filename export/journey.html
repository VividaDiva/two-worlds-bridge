What the row labels are

Each row is a key: one of 17 needs the reader can code a sentence as. The bold word is the key itself; the small line under it is the key’s plain-English gloss, written once for the whole study. Neither is a quotation. What the participant actually said is in the line cards above; the reader turned those words into keys, and the lanes follow the keys.
