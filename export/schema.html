The study as a database

Every table that the two pipelines read or write. The key pipeline turns a turn into annotation rows against a closed key list, aggregates them, and the builder writes a build of eight axis values. The wall pipeline keeps the words as sticky rows, groups them into themes, confirms each with a member_check, and writes one decision per theme against a part list that can grow.
