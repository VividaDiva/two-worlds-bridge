The Wall — the board everything plugs into

Today the AI hears in a list of 17 words, and the list was made from what it can build. Anything a person needs that isn't on the list is never heard. The Wall changes that in one move: the AI hears in plain language, on a board, and only meets the limits of its kit at the moment of building — out loud, with the people in the room.

What changes for the personThey talk as they do now. Before anything is built, they see their own words grouped and can say “not quite”.
What changes for the AIIt stops matching sentences to 17 words. It writes notes, groups them, answers every theme, and says what it can't do.
What changes for the kitIt's the starting point, not the ceiling. A theme with no part can become a part — a footway, a gate — when both people want it.

What every tool shares

Five rules

Hear first, limit laterLimit what can be built, never what can be heard.
Keep their wordsEvery need stays pinned to the sentence it came from.
Ask what it's forBuild for the reason behind the thing they named.
Make every ask a tapMost people type once. Choosing is cheaper than typing.
Say what you couldn'tA need silently dropped is the one thing a person can't fix.
