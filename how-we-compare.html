The rule

Both sides are lists of needs from the same fixed list of seventeen. The page puts the two lists side by side and looks at each need once.

carried

The need is in what the speaker meant and in what the listener interpreted.

missed

The need is in what the speaker meant, but not in what the listener interpreted.

new

The listener interpreted the need, but the speaker never meant it.

  • It compares needs, not words. The recording keeps no link from a word in the sentence to a need, so the page cannot say which words were understood — only which needs arrived.
  • Asking and ruling out count as the same need. If one side asks for “it does not move” and the other rules it out, the need still counts as carried. The walkthrough flags these.
  • The same idea said two ways counts as an addition. “Not loads and carts” and “one person's weight” are close in meaning, but they are different needs on the list, so the second shows as interpreted, never meant.
  • “Meant” is a self-report. It is the speaker's own answer when asked, after speaking — not a look inside it.
