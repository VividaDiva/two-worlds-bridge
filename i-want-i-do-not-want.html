I Want, I Do Not Want

Two people at odds over one crossing, one kind of sentence each, and a machine that builds from what it infers. Every case was played twenty-four times in each of four temperament pairings before this page appeared, and the table reports how often each outcome came up. Switch between cases to read them; watching one happen is optional.

These two are scripted, not people. In one room they answer each other; separated, they question the machine instead, and can come to want what is standing once they are told — but they never lie, never invent a sentence outside their script, never learn a word from each other, and only ever give ground toward what is already built. Apart, each knows only what it has said itself and whatever the machine has put in front of it.
