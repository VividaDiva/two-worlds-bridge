I Want, I Do Not Want

Two people at odds over one crossing, one kind of sentence each, and a machine that builds from what it infers.

These two are scripted, not people. They can ask the machine why, and can come to want what is standing once they are told — but they never lie, never invent a sentence outside their script, never learn a word from each other, and only ever give ground toward what is already built. Apart, each knows only what it has said itself and whatever the machine has put in front of it.
